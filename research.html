Working Papers

Nonparametric Test for the Equality of Conditional Distributions in Selection Models
With Cristine Pinto and Luis Alvarez — WP, 2026
This paper develops a nonparametric framework for testing equality of conditional distributions in selection models. The proposed hypothesis is fully distributional and encompasses implications of exogeneity and correct specification as special cases. The method is based on the Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD).

Work in Progress

Confidence Sets for Identified Sets via Classification-Based Metropolis-Hastings
This project proposes a likelihood free-based approach for settings with intractable likelihoods, leveraging recent advances in likelihood-free inference. By combining classification methods with a Metropolis–Hastings algorithm, we recover an implicit likelihood and draw samples from the posterior distribution. Confidence sets for the identified set are then constructed using quantiles of the sample classifier criterion evaluated at these draws.
